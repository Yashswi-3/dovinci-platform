The problem

The facts are there. Nobody is watching them.

  • Work is spread across systems. Connecting the dots still takes people.
  • Decisions change in conversations. The records can lag behind.
  • A report is due. It does not arrive. The silence goes unnoticed.

What it does

It keeps watch. Then it gets the work done.

DoVinci keeps working in the background. You do not have to open it.

  • Reads the context

    Reads messages, meetings, records and documents within each person's own permissions.

  • Spots the gaps

    Flags conflicting information across systems and warns when work starts to get held up.

  • Names the owner

    Alerts the person who needs to know and assigns the work to a named owner.

  • Moves work forward

    Adds notes to the right records, updates agreed facts after confirmation, and runs scheduled routines.

Actions that reach outside your company wait for a named person's approval.

Your control

You set the boundaries. Your people decide.

You decide who sees what and which actions DoVinci may take. It does not delete records.

How facts get confirmed

  1. DoVinci proposes an update based on what it finds.

  2. The people responsible confirm, reject or correct it.

  3. The record changes only after confirmation. Earlier versions remain readable.

  • Access stays restricted

    People see only information they already have permission to open.

  • Check access first

    Before reading starts, DoVinci flags information shared too widely so you can restrict access.

  • Actions inside your company

    DoVinci acts only from your approved list. Content it reads cannot expand that list.

  • Actions outside your company

    A named person reviews the evidence and decides before the action goes ahead.

A limit to know

Hidden instructions in documents remain an open problem. What DoVinci reads can lead it to a wrong conclusion. It can still act only from your approved list.
